💡 TIPS & TRICKS
===============
• Hold SUPER + Arrow keys for smooth window movement
• Use SUPER + SPACE to quickly float/unfloat windows
• SUPER + SHIFT + Q force-kills unresponsive applications
• ALT + TAB cycles through windows, SUPER + TAB cycles through workspaces
• Use SUPER + U to hide/show special workspace (scratchpad)
• SUPER + L toggles night light filter for eye comfort
• Print Screen + SUPER combos for various screenshot options
